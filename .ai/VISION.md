# Impossible Machine / OMEGA Vision

This file records direction, not completed functionality.

## Stable-Core direction

OMEGA makes Facts, Assumptions, Constraints, Unknowns, the Problem/Assumption Graph, WHY, BREAK IT, PROVE IT, WHAT IF, Evidence, Confidence, Uncertainty, and Falsification explicit. Simulation and experimentation may follow after evidence-backed Core maturity.

## Future layers

- Reality Compiler: translate Desired Reality plus Constraints into evaluable execution paths.
- WOS — World Operating System / THE WORLD HAS AN API: authorized objects and processes represented through State, Capabilities, Permissions, History, World Adapters, Permission Engine, World Terminal, and Adapter SDK.

## Research directions

GOD MODE, The Unasked Question, GENESIS, PROMETHEUS, and HORIZON are long-range R&D concepts. None is implemented or authorized by its presence here.
